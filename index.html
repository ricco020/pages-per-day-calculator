Pages per day calculator

Dividing pages by days only works for one book read every single day. Put in the whole stack, the page you are on now, your deadline and the days you actually read: you get the pages for each reading day, the page to reach each evening, the date you finish each book and how many minutes a day that is at your speed. Fell behind? Change "page I am on" and the plan is recalculated from today.

Days I read
Calendar file: one all-day event per reading day with the pages and the page to reach. Nothing is sent anywhere, the page runs in your browser and keeps your list in this browser only.

How the plan is calculated

Pages left = all the books added up, minus the pages already read in the first one. They are spread evenly over your reading days between the start and the deadline: by the end of reading day k you have read pages left times k divided by the number of reading days, rounded up, so you are never behind and the last day ends exactly on the last page. A book is finished on the first reading day whose target passes its last page.

Minutes per day use the speed you enter. 30 pages an hour is only a starting value: time yourself on 10 pages of the book you are reading, since dense non-fiction and small print go slower than a novel.
